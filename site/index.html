Open-source JavaScript library

Interaction bot detector

Tells people apart from bots and AI agents by how they use the mouse and keyboard, and catches automation that clicks without looking.

Source on GitHub Try the demo

What it is

Bots and AI agents that use a browser act like programs. They place the cursor right on a button instead of moving it there, release clicks within milliseconds, draw perfectly straight lines and type faster than any hand. This library records how a page is used and turns those traits into a verdict: human, bot, uncertain or not enough data, with the reasons behind it.

It has no dependencies and runs in the browser and in Node. From the keyboard it stores timings only, never which keys were pressed.

What it catches

Jumps and instant clicks

A cursor that appears on a button with no path to it, and clicks released in under 10 ms, as Playwright, Puppeteer and screenshot-based AI agents do.

Machine-made movement

Perfectly straight strokes at constant speed, and clicks on the exact centre of the element.

Machine typing

Keys held for a fraction of a millisecond, typing faster than a person, and fields filled from code with nothing human anywhere in the visit.

A trap for one button

At the moment someone is about to click it, the button moves. A person sees the change and aims again; a bot clicks where it was, or chases it faster than anyone can react.

How you use it

  1. Start a detector when the page loads and send its trace with the form.
  2. On your server, analyze the trace and decide. A bot never learns it was detected: it gets an ordinary HTTP error.
  3. Optionally, put the trap on your most important button, such as Save, Pay or Accept.

It catches automation that behaves like a program. It does not catch a bot built to imitate a person, and its thresholds still need checking against real users. The documentation explains every signal and every limitation.

Live demo

Use the form as you normally would, then try the same with Playwright or an AI agent. The panel shows the verdict and the signals as you go. When you reach the main button, a notice appears and pushes it down: that is the trap.
